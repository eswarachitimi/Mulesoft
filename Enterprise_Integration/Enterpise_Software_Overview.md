# Enterprise Software Overview

Enterprise software is large-scale computer software designed to meet the complex, operational needs of organizations rather than individual consumers.

---

## Key Characteristics

- **Scalability**: Built to handle thousands of concurrent users and massive volumes of data.  
- **Integration**: Connects different departments and databases so information flows smoothly across the entire organization.  
- **Centralization**: Replaces isolated spreadsheets with a single, unified source of truth for reporting and decision-making.  
- **Security & Reliability**: Features strict access controls, high uptime, and compliance with industry regulations.  

---

## Common Types

### 1. Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
Manages core business functions like finance, manufacturing, and supply chain in one place.

### 2. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
Tracks customer interactions, sales leads, and support requests.

### 3. Human Resource Management (HRM)
Handles payroll, hiring, benefits, and employee records.

### 4. Supply Chain Management (SCM)
Coordinates the flow of goods, data, and finances from supplier to customer.
